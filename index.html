Common questions

I can't hear any audio

The app uses your iPhone's built-in Greek voice. If your device doesn't have one installed, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Greek, and download a voice. Also check that your phone isn't on silent and the volume is turned up.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes — progress is saved only on your device, with no account or cloud sync. Reinstalling the app starts you back at the beginning.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Every lesson, phrase, and audio clip works fully offline once the app is installed.

Can I suggest a phrase or a new unit?

Yes, please do — email the address above with your idea.
